## 3. Legality of Emulation and Interoperability

Emulation is a well-established and legally recognized technology. Under United States federal jurisprudence and international copyright law:

1. **Clean-room reverse engineering and emulation** for the purpose of interoperability is lawful and protected as fair use (*Sony Computer Entertainment, Inc. v. Connectix Corp.*, 203 F.3d 596 (9th Cir. 2000); *Sega Enterprises Ltd. v. Accolade, Inc.*, 977 F.2d 1510 (9th Cir. 1992)).
2. Cannelloni acts solely as a **graphical user interface (frontend), launcher, and media organizer**. It executes open-source Libretro emulation cores and launches third-party standalone Android applications.
3. Cannelloni is developed completely independently of any proprietary console source code or unauthorized leaks.
